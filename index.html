Help & User Guide

This is one shared system for the whole office, divided into three branches — Admin Branch, Record Branch, and Accounts Branch. Everything you enter is saved online immediately and every other signed-in user sees the same information. An Administrator has full access everywhere; a Guest's access depends on which branch(es) their Administrator has granted them under "Users & Access" — a section without permission still shows for viewing and printing, its Add/Edit/Delete controls are just disabled.

Dashboard

A live overview of the whole system — everyone sees it on signing in.

  1. The stat cards at the top summarise cases, hearings, and pending items across all branches.
  2. "Upcoming hearings" lists everything due in the next 7 days, so nothing is missed.
  3. "Recently entered hearings" shows the latest proceedings added by anyone, useful as a quick daily check.

Cases — Main Register (Record Branch)

Register every case once here. Hearings and Results are entered separately against it, so the complete history builds up over time.

  1. Fill in Case No, Case Type, Case Title, Diary No, Filing Date, Bench/Court, and Assigned Employee, then press "Save case".
  2. Use "Search & filter cases" to quickly find a case by number, type, status, or assigned employee.
  3. The table's "Next Hearing" and "Latest Result" columns are filled in automatically from the Hearings and Results sections.
  4. "Export visible rows (CSV)" saves exactly the rows currently shown by your filters.

Hearings — Proceedings History (Record Branch)

Every time a case is heard, add one entry here against that case — never overwrite an old one.

  1. Pick the case first, then enter the hearing date, bench/court, and the proceedings/order for that date.
  2. Set the "Next Hearing Date" here too — this is what feeds the Dashboard's upcoming-hearings list.
  3. All past hearings for a case stay visible below in date order, so the full proceedings history is always at hand.

Results — Final & Interim Orders (Record Branch)

Record the outcome of a case here — a final judgment or an interim order.

  1. Pick the case, the result date, and describe the order or judgment in the remarks.
  2. Once a final result is recorded, update the case's Status (in the Cases section) to Decided/Disposed so it stops appearing as pending.

Employees (Admin Branch)

This is the master staff list, used to assign cases and hearings to the right person everywhere else in the system.

  1. Add every staff member here first — their name then appears in the "Assigned Employee" dropdown across Cases and other sections.
  2. Keep designations up to date, since they also appear on printed reports.

Dispatch — Dak In / Dak Out (Record Branch)

A complete daily record of incoming and outgoing correspondence (Dak), separate from the case register.

  1. Log every letter or file received ("Dak In") and every one sent out ("Dak Out"), with date, subject, and reference.
  2. Use Search & filter to trace a specific letter later by date or subject.

Token Register (Accounts Branch)

Track every bill sent to the Accounts Office for pre-audit or payment, from the day it's submitted until the cheque is received.

  1. Record the bill details and its token number as soon as it's submitted.
  2. Update its status (e.g. under audit, objection raised, cheque received) as it moves along, so its progress is always visible at a glance.

Reports

A combined case-load summary for any date range you choose.

  1. Pick a date range, then view the summary on screen.
  2. Use "Export report (Excel)" or "Print report" to share or file a copy.

Users & Access

This controls who can add, edit, or delete data, and in which branch.

  1. New accounts sign up from the sign-in screen and start as Guest (view-only everywhere).
  2. An Administrator ticks Admin Branch, Record Branch, and/or Accounts Branch for each Guest, matching exactly what that person needs to work on.
  3. A Guest can always view and print every section, even one they have no branch permission for — only the Add/Edit/Delete controls stay disabled there.

Backup, Restore & Access

Data is saved online immediately as everyone works, shared live across all signed-in users.

  1. Use "Backup (save file)" occasionally to keep an extra offline copy on this computer.
  2. "Restore from file" replaces all shared data with a backup file — Administrators only, since it affects everyone.

Frequently Asked Questions

I signed up but I can't add, edit, or delete anything — why?
New accounts start as Guest (view-only) for safety. An Administrator needs to grant you the relevant branch permission (Admin/Record/Accounts) under Users & Access before you can add, edit, or delete in that branch.
Why does this system have "branches" when the other Camp Office systems don't?
This system covers three different kinds of office work at once — Admin, Record, and Accounts — so permissions are split per branch instead of one simple Guest/Administrator switch, letting each Guest be granted exactly the access their actual job needs.
Which sections does each branch cover?
Admin Branch → Employees. Record Branch → Cases, Hearings, Results, Dispatch. Accounts Branch → Token Register. Reports and Backup/Restore are visible to everyone but Restore is Administrator-only.
How do I switch between English and Urdu?
Use the language selector at the top of the screen. It changes menus, labels, and buttons instantly for everyone's own screen — it doesn't change the data you've entered.
Where is my data stored — is it safe if this computer breaks?
Everything is stored online in this system's shared database, not on any one computer. If this computer is lost or damaged, the data is completely safe — just sign in again from any other computer.
Can more than one person use the system at the same time?
Yes. Everyone signed in sees the same live data, and changes made by one person appear for everyone else within a moment.
How does the Dashboard know which hearings are "upcoming"?
It reads the "Next Hearing Date" you set the last time you entered a hearing for each case, and lists anything falling in the next 7 days.
A case has been decided — what should I update?
Add the outcome in Results, then go back to that case in the Cases section and change its Status to Decided or Disposed so it drops off the pending lists.
How do I print a report or export it to Excel?
Open Reports, choose your date range, then use "Print report" or "Export report (Excel)" to share or file a copy.
Can I get case, hearing, or dispatch data into Excel?
Yes — most tables have an "Export visible rows (CSV)" or "Export (Excel)" button that saves exactly the rows currently shown by your filters, ready to open in Excel.
